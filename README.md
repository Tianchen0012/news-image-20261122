A 1024x1024 newspaper front page summing up the world news of
Sunday 22 November 2026: the COP31 climate talks collapse in Antalya,
Ukraine's sea drones sink the Russian warship Merkury off Benghazi,
Russian strikes kill 15 across the Kyiv region, and the factory fire
in Indonesia (3 dead, 36 missing as reported that Sunday).

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`; two runs write byte-identical output.
